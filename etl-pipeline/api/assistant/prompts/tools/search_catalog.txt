Search the research library catalog for relevant books.

The research catalog has been processed into a search index composed of the full text of every book divided into short text chunks. 

The search tool returns excerpts from up to 10 of the books with the most relevant content.

The search tool works by ranking all text chunks by the `ranking_query` and filtering out all text chunks that do not match the conditions in `filters`. Up to 10 books with the highest ranking text chunks are returned.
