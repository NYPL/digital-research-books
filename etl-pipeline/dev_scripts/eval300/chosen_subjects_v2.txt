9 Chosen Subjects v2 — GRIN 10k Sample (Structural Subject Diversity)
========================================================================

Designed to complement v1 by targeting structural diversity *within* the
subjects field itself: named person headings, pure geographic headings,
previously unrepresented form subdivisions, and deeply subdivided strings.
All 9 subjects are topically distinct from v1.

Structural diversity notes:
- Named person: "Napoleon 1769-1821" is a personal name heading with birth/death
  dates — exercises person-as-subject indexing and display.
- Geographic heading: "France -- History" has a place name as the PRIMARY heading
  entry (not a geographic subdivision), distinct from v1's place-as-subdivision
  subjects like "United States -- History -- Civil War, 1861-1865".
- New form subdivision: "-- Biography" has not appeared in v1; v1 used
  "-- Periodicals" and "-- Maps". Exercises form-facet handling.
- Deeply subdivided: "Meteorology -- Periodicals -- Statistics -- United States"
  has 4 components (3 " -- " separators), the maximum observed in GRIN 10k with
  a usable barcode count. Tests subject string parsing at depth.
